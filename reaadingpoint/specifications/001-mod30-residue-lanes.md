# Specification 001 — Mod-30 residue lanes

## Statement

For every prime p > 5,

gcd(p, 30) = 1

and therefore

p mod 30 ∈ {1, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19, 23, 29}.

## Interpretation

These eight residue classes are the Reading Point lanes generated by exclusion of divisibility by 2, 3, and 5.

No physical correspondence is asserted.

## Proposed use

Treat the eight-lane structure as a candidate discrete specification.

The next task is to ask whether any OpenWave model exposes an observable, symmetry class, degeneracy pattern, or state partition that can be compared against this eight-class structure.

## Falsifiability

A proposed correspondence must define:

- what OpenWave quantity is being partitioned;
- why eight classes are expected;
- how the classes are assigned;
- what would count as disagreement.

If those items cannot be specified, the correspondence does not advance to a test.
